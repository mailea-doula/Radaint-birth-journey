Use your BRAIN

Five simple questions to ask whenever something is offered or suggested — at any appointment, in labour, and beyond.

B
Benefits
What are the benefits of this, for me and for my baby?
R
Risks
What are the risks, and how likely are they?
A
Alternatives
Are there any other options we could try?
I
Intuition
What is my instinct telling me? How do I feel about it?
N
Nothing
What happens if we do nothing, or wait a little longer?

Helpful words to have ready

“Could we have a few minutes to talk this over?”

“Is this urgent, or do we have time to think?”

“Can you help us understand why this is being suggested?”
